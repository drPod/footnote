**Industry advisory revenue (derivation):**
US advised assets ≈ $35T (Cerulli's "intermediary-managed" pool, 40% of $88T investable) × ~0.85% blended advisory fee = **~$298B** in advisory revenue. The "$300B" figure checks out.

**Average revenue per advisor:**
$298B / 300,000 advisors = ~$1M. But that's misleading — wirehouse top quintile pulls ~$1.5-2M, mature RIA solo at $500-700K, and the long tail of insurance/B-D reps is far lower. Kitces consistently uses **$500-700K avg revenue per mature RIA advisor**.

**Tech budget per advisor:** Kitces' 2025 study: **4-6% of revenue → $20-40K/yr per advisor** all-in. ([Kitces Research 2025](https://www.kitces.com/kitces-report-independent-financial-advisor-technology-fintech-software-tools-research-2025/), [RightCapital summary](https://www.rightcapital.com/blog/kitces-report-technology/))

## 3. Bottom-Up TAM for an AI Advisor Product

Three SKUs, each priced from observed comps (Jump $75-120/seat/mo, Zocks similar, Practifi/Salesforce FSC enterprise).

### A. Paraplanner-seat SaaS @ $300/mo blended

Addressable seats:
- RIAs: ~100,000 paraplanners (1:3 ratio applied to ~300K advisors → but paraplanners are concentrated at the ~80K advisors at fee-only RIAs and large IBDs where the workflow exists)
- Realistic addressable: ~50,000 paraplanner seats in US

50,000 × $300 × 12 = **$180M/yr US paraplanner-seat TAM**

### B. Advisor-seat SaaS @ $450/mo blended ($300-600 range)

Addressable seats:
- Independent RIA advisors: ~110,000 (per Cerulli; subset of 300K total that are fee-only or hybrid RIA)
- Wirehouse advisors covered by enterprise contracts (counted separately below)
- Realistic standalone-seat addressable: ~125,000 advisors (RIA + IBD)

125,000 × $450 × 12 = **$675M/yr US advisor-seat TAM**

### C. Enterprise contracts @ $200K avg (range $50K-$500K)

- Top 50 RIAs (>$10B AUM): 50 × $300K = $15M
- Wirehouses (5 firms × ~$5M each — comparable to Salesforce FSC scale): $25M
- Mid-tier IBDs/RIAs (~500 firms × $80K avg): $40M

Enterprise total: **~$80M/yr US**

### Total bottom-up US TAM

| Layer | $/yr |
|---|---|
| Paraplanner seats | $180M |
| Advisor seats | $675M |
| Enterprise | $80M |
| **US TAM (current pricing)** | **~$935M/yr** |

If pricing rises to "AI-replaces-paraplanner" levels ($800-1,500/seat — i.e. take a fraction of the labor savings below), TAM expands to **$2.5-4B/yr US**. Global wealth-mgmt-AI adds ~70% more (UK + EMEA + APAC private banking) → **~$1.6B–$7B global**.

## 4. Labor Savings Cap on TAM (the real ceiling)

Loaded paraplanner cost: $77K median salary ([Glassdoor/PayScale 2025](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/paraplanner-salary-SRCH_KO0,11.htm)) × 1.4 benefits load = **~$108K/yr fully loaded → ~$52/hr**. Use $60-80/hr blended for senior associate/CFP-track work.

### Per-advisor annual labor savings if AI fully solves the workflow

| Workflow | Time saved | Frequency | Loaded cost | $/yr/advisor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HNW onboarding (fact-finder, plan setup, CRM, custodian forms) | 10 hr | 50 new clients/yr (boutique RIA avg) | $70/hr | $35,000 |
| Quarterly review prep (perf reports, agendas, briefings) | 4.5 hr | 100 clients × 4 = 400 reviews | $70/hr | $126,000 |
| Email + comms drafting | 5 hr/wk | 50 wk | $70/hr | $17,500 |
| Meeting notes + CRM entry | 10 hr/wk (Jump's internal claim) | 50 wk | $70/hr (advisor's own time at higher rate but blended) | $35,000 |
| Compliance documentation + IPS updates | 2 hr/wk | 50 wk | $70/hr | $7,000 |
| **Total per advisor (boutique RIA)** | | | | **~$220K/yr** |

That's huge. Realistic capture rate: AI never fully eliminates a paraplanner — call it **30-50% of theoretical savings** as the "willing to pay" zone → **$65-110K/yr/advisor** of value created.

### Industry-wide labor savings ceiling

Apply to addressable advisors only (not all 300K — many don't have this workflow):
- 110K independent RIA advisors × $80K avg captured value = **$8.8B/yr** theoretical labor-savings pool
- 60K wirehouse advisors × $50K avg (lower because much is centralized) = **$3.0B/yr**
- **Total US labor-savings-justified ceiling: ~$12B/yr**

A vendor typically captures **15-30%** of demonstrated savings as price → **$1.8-3.6B/yr in price-realizable TAM** even after labor justification — closer to the upper bound of section 3.

## Bottom Line

> **TAM for US wealth-mgmt-AI is $0.9B–$3.6B per year, of which ~$1.8–3.6B is labor-savings-justified at 15-30% capture of the $12B/yr theoretical labor-savings pool.** Today the category does ~$80-120M ARR (Jump + Zocks + smaller). Path to $300-500M ARR by 2028 is plausible; comp companies (Harvey, Abridge) hit $100-190M ARR within 3 years of product-market fit at $5-11B valuations, suggesting the eventual category winner in advisor-AI is an **$8-15B outcome**.
